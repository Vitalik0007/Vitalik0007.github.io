About the Game

ViceHustle is a mobile game built in Unity 6 and submitted to Google Play. The project focuses on responsive arcade/action gameplay, progressive enemy waves with dynamic modifiers, in-game economy, and clean mobile UX.

My Role

Sole Unity developer responsible for the entire architecture, gameplay systems, UI animations, save/encryption layer, localization, monetization (AdMob & IAP), Google Sign-In integration, custom Unity Editor tooling, and Google Play release preparation.

Technologies & Architecture

  • Unity 6 (6000.3) & URP: Modular gameplay systems, base classes, and event-driven communication.
  • ScriptableObjects Configs: Data-driven balancing for enemies, modifiers, progression, and currency.
  • Enemy Spawner & Modifiers: Configurable wave spawning and runtime enemy modifier system.
  • Save & SafeTypes: Persistent player progression with protected value types.
  • Monetization & Services: Google AdMob, Unity In-App Purchasing (IAP), Google Sign-In, New Input System, and Localization.
